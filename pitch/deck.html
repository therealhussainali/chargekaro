One network · two sides

One app.
Two roles.
One flow.

A driver needs a confirmed place to plug in. A host already has the plug. ChargeKaro provides the listing, the booking and the meter between them.

Demand side · 3 steps
Drivers
Set your route — enter your EV, destination and target charge; stops are ranked by plug type and detour.
Reserve a slot — power, plug, tariff and amenities up front, with the cost shown before booking.
Start charging — share a one-time OTP with the host and watch energy and fare meter live.
Supply side · 3 steps
Hosts
List your charger — publish an existing wallbox with power, plug type and guest amenities.
Set tariff & hours — price per kWh, availability window and driveway notes.
Approve bookings — accept or decline each live request, paid per completed session.

05 · THE FLOW
End to end

How a charge happens.

The host lists the charger and sets tariff once (Host Steps 1–2). Every booking then runs this five-step loop:

STEP 01
Driver sets route
EV profile, destination and target charge.
STEP 02
App ranks stops
Compatible plugs, detour, speed and availability.
STEP 03
Driver reserves
Duration, instant estimate, request to the host.
STEP 04
Host approves
Live request with driver ETA and payout.
STEP 05
Charge & settle
OTP handoff, live metering, host payout.
Driver outcome
A reserved slot at a known price per kWh, with the fare metered live.
Host outcome
Paid per kWh from a charger that already exists — with control over price and access.

Unit economics

Where the margin comes from.

The host sets a price per kWh above grid cost and keeps the difference. The driver still pays less than public DC fast charging — and both sides see the numbers before committing.
